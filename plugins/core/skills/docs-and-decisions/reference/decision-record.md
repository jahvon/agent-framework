# NNNN — Short title naming the problem and the choice

## Context

The situation and the forces at play, in two to five sentences: what problem needed a decision,
what constrained it, and why now.

## Options

- **Option A** — one line on what it is; its main cost.
- **Option B** — one line; its main cost.

## Decision

We chose **Option X** because ⟨the deciding reason⟩.

## Consequences

- What becomes easier or is now required of other code.
- What becomes harder, and the tradeoff accepted.
- What would make us revisit this.
